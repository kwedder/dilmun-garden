# Clinical handbook: common analgesics

- aspirin | treats | fever
- aspirin | causes | stomach irritation
- ibuprofen | is_a | NSAID
- ibuprofen | treats | inflammation
- ibuprofen | treats | pain
- fever | part_of | inflammatory response

Dosing belongs to the prescriber, not to this handbook.
