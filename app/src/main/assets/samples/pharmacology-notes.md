# Pharmacology notes: salicylates

Willow bark was used against fever and pain long before anyone knew why.
The active ingredient is salicin, which the body turns into salicylic acid.

- aspirin | treats | fever
- aspirin | treats | pain
- aspirin | is_a | NSAID
- willow bark | contains | salicin
- salicin | source_of | salicylic acid

Aspirin blocks the enzymes that make prostaglandins. That is why it lowers
fever and eases pain, and also why it irritates the stomach.

- aspirin | causes | stomach irritation
